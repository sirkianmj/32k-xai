About this platform

A research tool for causal, individual-level interrogation of emergent social behaviour.

What it is

32k-XAI is a research platform that simulates 32,000 cognitive agents. Each agent has a transparent, mathematically explicit brain — a small set of equations whose parameters come from real human psychometric data.

No neural networks. No large language models. Nothing hidden. Every decision can be traced back to a named parameter with a documented source.

What you can ask it

  • What happens to cooperation when resources become scarce?
  • Does psychological distress flatten or sharpen economic inequality?
  • How does social isolation change trust and reciprocity?
  • For a specific agent at a specific tick: why did it make the choice it made?

The science behind it

The platform reproduces five published human benchmarks within their standard errors: ultimatum offer rate, ultimatum rejection rate, trust-game send rate, trust-game return rate, and wealth Gini. All at full 32,000-agent scale.

This dashboard shows you those numbers live, along with the individual decisions behind them.
